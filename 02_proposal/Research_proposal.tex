\section{Research Question and Hypothesis}

\subsection{Research question}


\textbf{RQ 1:} How does NIMA's aesthetic quality assessment correspend to human aesthetic judgement of AI-generated landscape images?
\smallbreak
\textbf{RQ 2:} How does the agreement between NIMA and human aesthetic judgement differ across image generators?
\subsection{Hypothesis}

\textbf{H1:} Higher NIMA scores are expected to be associated with higher human aesthetic ratings of AI-generated landscape images, resulting in a positive correlation between the two measures.
\smallbreak
\textbf{H2:} The relationship between NIMA aesthetic scores and mean human ratings is expected to vary across image generators, even when the generators are evaluated on the same set of landscape prompts.
